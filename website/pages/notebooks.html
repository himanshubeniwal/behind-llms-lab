How to run them

nothing to install

Google Colab

Press Open in Colab on a notebook. For notebooks 5 and 6, switch to a T4 GPU under Runtime > Change runtime type. Notebook 4 is fine on a CPU. You need a Google account.

on the cluster

JupyterHub

After the setup, open jupyterhub.hpc.tu-dresden.de, start a server and choose the kernel LLM Lab (venv-llm). The notebooks are in ~/behind-llms-lab/notebooks.

offline

Your laptop

Clone the repository and install torch transformers matplotlib jupyter. Without a GPU the notebooks choose smaller settings by themselves.

How they fit together

Each notebook starts where the one before stopped, and each ends with exercises.

  1. Tokens and a bigram language modelText becomes numbers. A model that only counts which character follows which reaches a loss of about 2.5. You meet cross-entropy, perplexity and the temperature.
  2. Self-attention and a tiny GPTEvery position may look at all earlier ones. Same data, same loss, same training loop, and much better text. Then you look at what the attention heads learned.
  3. Pretrained LLMs for text analysisReal models from Hugging Face, used as measuring tools: tokenizers across languages, perplexity, surprisal, zero-shot labels and sentence embeddings.

They follow the warm-up notebooks 0 to 3b from the first crash course (regression, arbitrary functions, MNIST, autograd and neurons). If those ideas are new to you, read the crash course page first.

The same model runs on the cluster. The tiny GPT of notebook 5 is exercise 4 of the HPC lab, and the chat model at the end of notebook 6 is exercise 6. On an A100 or H100 you can train a model ten times bigger in the same time.
